## Robotics = Physical AI

Robotics represents the physical realization of artificial intelligence, bridging digital computation with mechanical actuation to interact directly with the physical world. As software algorithms achieve sophisticated perception, decision-making, and natural language understanding, robotics provides the hardware chassis, sensors, and actuators necessary to execute autonomous physical tasks in unstructured environments. From industrial automation and logistics to autonomous mobility and humanoid systems, the convergence of advanced machine learning and robotics transforms software intelligence into physical capability, enabling machines to manipulate objects, navigate dynamic spaces, and execute complex workflows without human intervention.
