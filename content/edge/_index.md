# Edge AI & HPC

**Edge AI** shifts machine learning inference and lightweight training directly to endpoint hardware and local gateways, eliminating network round-trip latency, reducing bandwidth consumption, and ensuring autonomous operation disconnected from the cloud. Edge AI is possible due to new hardware, more compact and more efficient.
